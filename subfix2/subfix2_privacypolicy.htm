SubFix2 privacy policy

Data in SubFix2

My privacy is very important to me; as is yours. SubFix2 stores and processes all data on your Mac. No data is transferred or shared. You can open and save the reformatted subtitle file to your local storage device only.

App Store

Because SubFix2 is distributed through the App Store and runs on macOS, use of SubFix2 is also subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. This policy does not address any data that Apple may collect which may include but not be limited to usage statistics and crash reports.

Support exchange

If you contact with me using the Issue function, you are using a public service managed by Microsoft company. As such, all support conversation is bound to the terms of Github Policy.
