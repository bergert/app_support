KNX privacy policy

Data in KNX

The KNX app saves the rooms and devices to your iCloud, so that when you open the app on another device so you don't have to re-create the KNX devices.

No other data is transferred or shared.

App Store

Because KNX is distributed through the App Store, its use is also subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. This policy does not address any data that Apple may collect which may include but not be limited to usage statistics and crash reports.

Support exchange

If you contact with me using the Issue function, you are using a public service managed by Microsoft company. As such, all support conversation is bound to the terms of Github Policy.
