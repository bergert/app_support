TerminalConsole privacy policy

Data

My privacy is very important to me; as is yours. TerminalConsole stores and processes all data on your Mac. No data is transferred or shared. You export the serial port activity to clipboard (paste), or save it to a file on your device.

App Store

Because TerminalConsole is distributed through the App Store and runs on macOS, its use is also subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. This policy does not address any data that Apple may collect which may include but not be limited to usage statistics and crash reports.

Support exchange

If you contact with me using the Issue function, you are using a public service managed by Microsoft company. As such, all support conversation is bound to the terms of Github Policy.
